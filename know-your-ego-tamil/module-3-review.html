    Healthy Ego vs. Inflated Ego vs. Low Self-Worth — Review

    A quick recap of what this module covered, before you take the live assessment.

    Recognising the Middle Ground: Healthy Ego

    A healthy ego is quietly confident, not loud.

    The Two Extremes: Inflated Ego and Low Self-Worth

    An inflated ego thinks too highly of itself, resists feedback, and always needs praise — but underneath, it is often fragile and afraid of being “found out.” Low self-worth, the opposite extreme, always seeks approval, apologises too much, and struggles to say no.
