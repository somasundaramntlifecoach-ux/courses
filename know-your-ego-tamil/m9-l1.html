    The STOP Technique and the 24-Hour Rule

    Practical Techniques to Manage Your Ego

    📖 Detailed Explanation

    The STOP technique is a four-step, in-the-moment tool: Stop what you're doing, Take a breath, Observe what's happening in your body and mind, and Proceed with intention. It interrupts the automatic ego reaction long enough for your rational, Adult mind to step back in. Combine it with the 24-hour rule — waiting a full day before sending an angry email, resigning, or making a big announcement — and these two techniques handle most ego-driven mistakes.

    🌳 Metaphor

    STOP is like the pause button on a remote control. It doesn't switch off the movie (your emotions) — it just freezes the frame long enough for you to decide what happens next, instead of the scene playing out on its own.

    👤 Real-Life Example

    After a frustrating client email, Nithya wrote a sharp reply, then used the 24-hour rule and saved it as a draft instead of sending it. The next morning, she reread it, softened the tone, and added two useful suggestions. The client later thanked her for handling a tense situation so professionally.

    💡 Practical Tips
    • Keep the STOP steps written somewhere visible (phone lock screen, desk note) until they become automatic.
    • Never send an emotional message in the same hour you wrote it.
    • Practice STOP in small, low-stress moments (traffic, minor annoyances) so it's ready for the big moments.
    🧭 Coach's Guidance

    Write the STOP steps and the 24-hour rule on a small card, and keep it in your wallet or workspace for the first 30 days. Seeing a reminder in front of you greatly increases how often a new habit actually gets used under real pressure.

    Live Worksheet

    Reflection Exercise

    Describe one upcoming situation where you expect your ego to be triggered. Write out exactly how you will apply STOP and the 24-hour rule when it happens.
